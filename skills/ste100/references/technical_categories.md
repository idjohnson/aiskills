# Technical Noun & Technical Verb Categories (Rules 1.5 & 1.12)

In ASD-STE100 Issue 9, words that do not appear in the general dictionary (Part 2) can still be used without violating STE if they qualify under designated **Technical Noun (TN)** or **Technical Verb (TV)** categories.

## Technical Noun (TN) Categories (Rule 1.5)

To qualify as a Technical Noun, an unapproved word must belong to one of these 7 recognized categories:

1. **Names of Components and Parts**:
   - Examples: *gasket, O-ring, bushing, flange, piston, resistor, busbar, bracket, fastener, turbine*.
2. **Names of Assemblies and Systems**:
   - Examples: *engine, gearbox, inverter, landing gear, flight control computer, hydraulics, elevator*.
3. **Names of Tools and Test Equipment**:
   - Examples: *torque wrench, multimeter, caliper, oscilloscope, extractor, adapter, gauge*.
4. **Names of Materials and Consumables**:
   - Examples: *aluminum, titanium, seal oil, grease, primer, isopropyl alcohol, threadlocker*.
5. **Names of Facilities, Infrastructure, and Locations**:
   - Examples: *hangar, test bench, cleanroom, workshop, cockpit, bay, cabin*.
6. **Units of Measurement**:
   - Examples: *millimeter (mm), bar, kilopascal (kPa), volt (V), ampere (A), hertz (Hz), degree Celsius (°C)*.
7. **Scientific, Mathematical, or Medical Terms**:
   - Examples: *viscosity, frequency, voltage, corrosion, oxidation, contamination, friction*.

### Rules for Technical Nouns:
- Do not use Technical Nouns as verbs (Rule 1.7).
- Select short and simple Technical Nouns (Rule 1.9).
- Maintain 100% terminology consistency (Rule 1.11).

---

## Technical Verb (TV) Categories (Rule 1.12)

To qualify as a Technical Verb, an unapproved verb must belong to one of these recognized manufacturing or maintenance process categories:

1. **Machining and Manufacturing Operations**:
   - Examples: *drill, mill, solder, weld, ream, rivet, etch, braze, countersink, chamfer, grind*.
2. **Specific Assembly and Securing Operations**:
   - Examples: *torque, safety-wire, crimp, stake, swage*.

### Rules for Technical Verbs:
- Do not use Technical Verbs as nouns (Rule 1.13).
- Use Technical Verbs only in permitted simple tenses (imperative, simple present, simple past).
