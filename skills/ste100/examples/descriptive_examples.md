# ASD-STE100 Descriptive Writing Examples

## Example 1: System Function Description

### Original Non-STE Text (Violations: >25 words, multi-topic paragraph, passive voice, unapproved words):
> "The primary auxiliary power supply unit serves to provide secondary electrical power to the emergency busbar system when the primary AC generators experience total failure, thereby allowing critical flight navigation instruments to continue functioning for a period of up to thirty minutes."

### STE Analysis & Rule Violations:
1. Sentence length: 39 words (Exceeds 25-word limit) [Rule 6.3].
2. `serves to provide`: Nominalization/wordy. Use `supplies` [Rule 3.7].
3. `primary auxiliary power supply unit`: 6-word noun cluster (Exceeds 3-word limit) [Rule 2.1].
4. `thereby allowing`: Unapproved connecting phrase, gerund `-ing` clause [Rule 3.5, Rule 4.4].

### STE Approved Rewritten Version:
> The auxiliary power unit (APU) supplies electrical power to the emergency busbar. If the main AC generators fail, the APU operates automatically. The APU supplies power to the critical navigation instruments for up to 30 minutes.

## Example 2: Component Description

### Original Non-STE Text:
> "The pressure relief valve is comprised of a stainless steel housing enclosing a spring-loaded piston assembly; it functions to prevent system overpressurization."

### STE Approved Rewritten Version:
> The pressure relief valve has a stainless steel housing and a spring-loaded piston. The valve prevents high pressure in the system.
