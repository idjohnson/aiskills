# ASD-STE100 Safety Instructions Examples (Section 7)

## Correct WARNING Examples (Injury / Death Risk)

### Example 1: High Voltage Risk
- **Non-STE**: *Caution: Electric shock hazard exists when working on live terminals.*
- **STE Approved**:
  `WARNING: DO NOT TOUCH THE LIVE ELECTRICAL TERMINALS. HIGH VOLTAGE CAN CAUSE SEVERE ELECTRIC SHOCK OR DEATH.`

### Example 2: Hot Surface Risk
- **Non-STE**: *Be careful as the engine tailpipe might be hot after shutdown.*
- **STE Approved**:
  `WARNING: DO NOT TOUCH THE EXHAUST TAILPIPE IMMEDIATELY AFTER ENGINE SHUTDOWN. HIGH TEMPERATURE CAN CAUSE SEVERE BURNS.`

---

## Correct CAUTION Examples (Equipment Damage Risk)

### Example 1: Chemical Solvent Damage
- **Non-STE**: *Note that solvent cleaners can erode the acrylic windshield surface.*
- **STE Approved**:
  `CAUTION: DO NOT USE SOLVENT CLEANERS ON THE ACRYLIC WINDSHIELD. SOLVENTS CAN DAMAGE THE PLASTIC SURFACE.`

### Example 2: Over-torquing Fasteners
- **Non-STE**: *Avoid excessive torque on the aluminum screws to prevent thread stripping.*
- **STE Approved**:
  `CAUTION: DO NOT APPLY MORE THAN 12 NM OF TORQUE TO THE ALUMINUM SCREWS. EXCESSIVE TORQUE CAN DAMAGE THE THREADS.`

---

## Correct NOTE Examples (Information Only - NO Instructions)

- **Non-STE Incorrect NOTE**: *NOTE: Disconnect the ground cable first before removing the battery.* (INCORRECT: Gives an instruction!)
- **STE Approved NOTE**: *NOTE: The ground cable is marked with a black stripe.*
- **STE Approved Step**: *Disconnect the ground cable before you remove the battery.*
