# Section 2: Multi-word Nouns (Rules 2.1 – 2.3)

## Rule 2.1: Maximum 3 Nouns in a Noun Cluster
Write multi-word nouns (noun clusters) of **no more than three words**.
When four or more nouns or modifiers are placed together, the relationship between words becomes ambiguous.

- **Non-STE (4 words)**: *overhead fuel tank cap*
- **STE Approved**: *cap of the overhead fuel tank* or *overhead fuel-tank cap*

- **Non-STE (5 words)**: *flight control system circuit breaker*
- **STE Approved**: *circuit breaker of the flight control system*

## Rule 2.2: Exceptions for Official Technical Names
When an official registered technical name (e.g. part name from a Illustrated Parts Catalog or engineering drawing) has more than three words, you can write it in full if necessary. However, break it up with prepositions or hyphens wherever possible.

- **Official Name**: *MAIN LANDING GEAR DOOR ACTUATOR CYLINDER*
- **STE Preferred**: *actuator cylinder of the main landing gear door*

## Rule 2.3: Clear Noun Modification with Hyphens and Prepositions
Use hyphens or prepositions to show clearly which word modifies which noun in a multi-word noun structure.

- **Ambiguous**: *high temperature sensor wire*
- **STE Approved**: *wire of the high-temperature sensor* or *sensor wire for high temperature*
