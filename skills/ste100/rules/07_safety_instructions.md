# Section 7: Safety Instructions (Rules 7.1 – 7.3)

## Rule 7.1: Signal Words & Hazard Levels
Use an applicable signal word to identify the level of risk:
1. **WARNING**: Used for hazard levels where personal injury or death can occur.
2. **CAUTION**: Used for hazard levels where damage to equipment or property can occur.
3. **NOTE**: Used to highlight essential explanatory or non-hazard information (NO instructions allowed in NOTES).

Always place `WARNING` and `CAUTION` instructions **BEFORE** the procedural step to which they apply.

## Rule 7.2: Clear & Accurate Hazard Command or Condition
Start a safety instruction with a clear command or condition statement.
State clearly **what action to do or not do**.

- **Example**: `WARNING: DO NOT TOUCH THE HIGH-VOLTAGE TERMINALS.`

## Rule 7.3: Risk & Consequence Explanation
Give a clear explanation to show the risk or possible result of ignoring the safety instruction.

### Standard Safety Structure:
`SIGNAL WORD: COMMAND / CONDITION. HAZARD / CONSEQUENCE.`

### Correct Examples:
- **WARNING**: `WARNING: DO NOT LOOK DIRECTLY AT THE LASER BEAM. EYE DAMAGE CAN OCCUR.`
- **CAUTION**: `CAUTION: USE ONLY THE APPROVED SOLVENT. OTHER SOLVENTS CAN DAMAGE THE RUBBER SEALS.`
