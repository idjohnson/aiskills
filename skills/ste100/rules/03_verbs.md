# Section 3: Verbs (Rules 3.1 – 3.7)

## Rule 3.1: Approved Verb Forms Only
Use only the verb forms given in the dictionary (Part 2). Do not construct unapproved verb tenses or forms.

## Rule 3.2: Allowed Verb Forms & Tenses
Use **only** these 5 specific verb forms and tenses:

1. **Imperative (Command Form)**:
   - Used in procedures and instructions.
   - Example: *Remove the bolt.*, *Turn the switch to OFF.*

2. **Present Simple (Active Voice)**:
   - Used in descriptive text and condition statements.
   - Example: *The green light shows that power is ON.*

3. **Past Simple (Active Voice)**:
   - Used to describe past actions or historical events.
   - Example: *The technician installed the new seal.*

4. **Future Simple (`will` + Infinitive)**:
   - Used to state future occurrences or consequences.
   - Example: *The motor will stop when you press the button.*

5. **Past Participle as an Adjective**:
   - Used ONLY as a descriptive modifier before or after a noun.
   - Example: *Replace the damaged seal.*, *The valve remains closed.*

## Rule 3.3: Past Participle Usage Limits
Do not use past participles to create passive voice or perfect tenses (*has removed*, *had opened*). Use past participle **only as an adjective**.

- **Non-STE (Passive / Perfect)**: *The filter has been cleaned by the technician.*
- **STE Approved (Active)**: *The technician cleaned the filter.*
- **STE Approved (Adjective)**: *Install the cleaned filter.*

## Rule 3.4: No Auxiliary Verbs for Complex Constructions
Do not use auxiliary verbs to make complex verb constructions.
- **FORBIDDEN MODAL AUXILIARIES**: *should, could, might, may, shall, would, ought to*.
- **ALLOWED**:
  - `must` (to express an obligation or requirement).
  - `can` (to express capability or permission).
  - `will` (to express future time).

- **Non-STE**: *The cover should be removed carefully.*
- **STE Approved**: *You must remove the cover carefully.* or *Remove the cover carefully.*

## Rule 3.5: Restrictions on "-ing" Verb Forms (Gerunds / Participles)
Use the **"-ing"** form of a verb **ONLY**:
1. As an approved Technical Noun (TN) (e.g., *parking, warning, lighting, wiring*).
2. As a modifier in a Technical Noun (e.g., *cleaning agent, locking washer, retaining ring*).

**NEVER use "-ing" forms to build continuous tenses or participle clauses**:
- **Non-STE (Continuous)**: *The pump is running.* -> **STE Approved**: *The pump operates.*
- **Non-STE (Clause)**: *After removing the bolt, check the seal.* -> **STE Approved**: *Remove the bolt, then check the seal.*

## Rule 3.6: Active Voice vs. Passive Voice
Use the **active voice**.
- **Procedural Writing**: Passive voice is **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN**.
- **Descriptive Writing**: Passive voice is permitted **ONLY** when the person or agent performing the action is unknown, unimportant, or obvious.

- **Non-STE (Procedural Passive)**: *The valve is opened by turning the knob.*
- **STE Approved (Imperative/Active)**: *Turn the knob to open the valve.*

## Rule 3.7: Use Verbs to Describe Actions
Use an approved verb to describe an action, rather than a noun or noun phrase. Avoid heavy nominalization.

- **Non-STE**: *Perform an inspection of the seal.*
- **STE Approved**: *Inspect the seal.*

- **Non-STE**: *Carry out the installation of the unit.*
- **STE Approved**: *Install the unit.*
