# Section 4: Sentences (Rules 4.1 – 4.5)

## Rule 4.1: Short & Clear Sentences
Write short, direct, and clear sentences. Keep thought structures simple. Express one main idea per sentence.

## Rule 4.2: No Omitted Words & No Contractions
Do not omit words (such as articles, pronouns, or prepositions) or use contractions to make sentences shorter.

- **NO CONTRACTIONS**: Do not use *don't, doesn't, can't, won't, it's, isn't, shouldn't*. Write in full: *do not, does not, cannot, will not, it is, is not*.
- **NO TELEGRAPHIC STYLE**: Do not omit articles (*the, a, an*).
  - **Non-STE (Telegraphic)**: *Check oil level in tank.*
  - **STE Approved**: *Check the oil level in the tank.*

## Rule 4.3: Vertical Lists for Complex Text
Use a vertical list to explain complex steps, multi-item conditions, or long lists of components.

- **Non-STE**: *To start the engine, open valve A, close valve B, check pressure C, and turn key D.*
- **STE Approved**:
  * To start the engine, do these steps in sequence:
    1. Open valve A.
    2. Close valve B.
    3. Check pressure C.
    4. Turn key D.

## Rule 4.4: Approved Connecting Words & Phrases
Use approved connecting words and phrases to connect sentences that contain related thoughts:
- `and`, `or`, `but`, `because`, `so that`, `if`, `when`, `before`, `after`, `then`, `also`.

Avoid unapproved or ambiguous connectors such as *in as much as*, *whereby*, *thereby*, *in order to* (use *to*).

## Rule 4.5: Explicit Articles & Demonstrative Adjectives
When applicable, place an article (*the, a, an*) or a demonstrative adjective (*this, these*) before a noun to avoid ambiguity.

- **Non-STE**: *Replace damaged bolt.*
- **STE Approved**: *Replace the damaged bolt.* or *Replace this damaged bolt.*
