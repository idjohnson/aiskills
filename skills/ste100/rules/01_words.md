# Section 1: Words (Rules 1.1 – 1.14)

## Rule 1.1: Approved Words & Vocabulary Categories
Use words that are:
1. Approved in the dictionary (Part 2).
2. Technical Nouns (TN) in allowed categories (Rule 1.5).
3. Technical Verbs (TV) in allowed categories (Rule 1.12).
4. Short relationship words (articles, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions).

## Rule 1.2: Part of Speech Compliance
Use approved words from the dictionary **only as the specified part of speech**.
- **Correct**: *Use a CLEAN cloth.* (`CLEAN` as adjective)
- **Incorrect**: *CLEAN the valve with solvent.* (unless `CLEAN` is approved as verb or used as Technical Verb; if `CLEAN` is listed as verb in dictionary, use it as verb).

## Rule 1.3: Approved Meanings Only
Use approved words **only with their approved meanings** specified in Column 2 of the dictionary.
- **Example**: `OIL` (n) is approved only as a liquid lubricant. Do not use `OIL` to mean petroleum fuel.

## Rule 1.4: Approved Forms of Verbs and Adjectives
Use only the approved forms of verbs and adjectives provided in the dictionary or rule definitions (e.g. simple present, simple past, imperative, past participle as adjective).

## Rule 1.5: Technical Noun (TN) Categories
You can use unapproved words as **Technical Nouns (TN)** if they fall into one of the following recognized technical categories:
1. **Names of components / parts**: *flange, gasket, resistor, busbar, bracket, piston*.
2. **Names of assemblies / subassemblies**: *engine, gearbox, landing gear, inverter*.
3. **Names of tools / equipment**: *multimeter, torque wrench, caliper, oscilloscope*.
4. **Names of materials / substances**: *aluminum, titanium, seal oil, grease, sealant*.
5. **Names of facilities / locations**: *workshop, test bench, cleanroom, hangar*.
6. **Units of measurement**: *millimeter, bar, kilopascal, volt, ampere, degree Celsius*.
7. **Medical, scientific, or mathematical terms**: *corrosion, viscosity, frequency, voltage*.

## Rule 1.6: Unapproved Words Exception
Use a word that is not approved in the dictionary **only** when it is a valid Technical Noun (TN) or Technical Verb (TV).

## Rule 1.7: No Technical Nouns as Verbs
Do not use words that are Technical Nouns as verbs.
- **Correct**: *Apply a layer of tape.*
- **Incorrect**: *Tape the wire to the panel.*

## Rule 1.8: Company & Industry Approved Terminology
Use Technical Nouns that are officially approved in your company, industry, or project field. Avoid inventing unofficial terms.

## Rule 1.9: Short & Easy Technical Nouns
When selecting a Technical Noun, choose the shortest and easiest term to understand.
- **Prefer**: *wire* over *electrical conductor line*.

## Rule 1.10: No Slang, Jargon, or Regional Words
Do not use regional words, slang, or non-standard jargon as Technical Nouns (e.g., *cheater bar*, *pigtail*).

## Rule 1.11: One Word, One Meaning (Consistency)
Do not use different technical nouns for the same item. Maintain 100% terminology consistency throughout the document set.
- **Do not mix**: *switch*, *toggle*, and *selector* for the exact same component.

## Rule 1.12: Technical Verb (TV) Categories
You can use unapproved verbs as **Technical Verbs (TV)** if they describe specific manufacturing or maintenance processes in these categories:
1. **Manufacturing / Machining operations**: *drill, mill, solder, weld, ream, rivet, etch, braze, countersink*.
2. **Specific physical operations**: *torque, safety-wire, crimp, stake*.

## Rule 1.13: No Technical Verbs as Nouns
Do not use Technical Verbs as nouns.
- **Correct**: *Drill a hole in the plate.*
- **Incorrect**: *Make a drill in the plate.*

## Rule 1.14: American English Spelling
Use American English spelling rules (as defined in Merriam-Webster) unless official customer directives mandate otherwise.
- **STE / American**: *color, center, analyze, check, meter, gray, defense*.
- **Non-STE / British**: *colour, centre, analyse, cheque, metre, grey, defence*.
