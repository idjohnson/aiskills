# Section 5: Procedural Writing (Rules 5.1 – 5.5)

## Rule 5.1: Maximum 20 Words per Sentence
Write short sentences in procedural text. Use a **maximum of 20 words** in each procedural sentence.

- Count every word including articles, prepositions, and numbers.
- If a sentence exceeds 20 words, split it into two or more shorter sentences or convert it into a vertical list.

## Rule 5.2: One Instruction per Sentence
Write **only one instruction** in each sentence, unless two or more actions occur at the same time.

- **Non-STE**: *Remove the cover and clean the gasket.* (Two separate sequential actions)
- **STE Approved**:
  1. *Remove the cover.*
  2. *Clean the gasket.*
- **Simultaneous Exception**: *Push the red button and turn the selector switch at the same time.*

## Rule 5.3: Imperative (Command) Form
Write instructions in the **imperative form** (command verb at the beginning of the sentence).

- **Non-STE**: *The technician must disconnect the cable.*
- **STE Approved**: *Disconnect the cable.*

- **Non-STE**: *Cleaning of the filter is required.*
- **STE Approved**: *Clean the filter.*

## Rule 5.4: Conditions First
When an instruction depends on a condition, **start the instruction with the condition clause** (*If...*, *When...*).

- **Non-STE**: *Press the emergency stop button if the temperature exceeds 90 °C.*
- **STE Approved**: *If the temperature is higher than 90 °C, press the emergency stop button.*

## Rule 5.5: Notes Are for Information Only
Write **NOTE** entries only to give information, **NEVER to give instructions**.

- **Non-STE NOTE**: *NOTE: Make sure to torque the bolt to 25 Nm.* (Instruction disguised as Note!)
- **STE Approved NOTE**: *NOTE: The torque value depends on the ambient temperature.*
- **STE Approved Step**: *Torque the bolt to 25 Nm.*
